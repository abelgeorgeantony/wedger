Wedger

Personal accounting that stays in your browser.

There is no server and no account to make. Your files are saved on this device. If you sign in with Google, they are also copied to your own Drive, so you can open them on your other devices.

  • Add transactions with a formWedger checks that each one adds up before it saves.
  • Bring in your bank statementsImport a CSV and see exactly what will be added before you confirm.
  • See where your money wentBalances, income and spending, cash flow and more, from the Reports menu.
  • Browse by dateThe Timeline shows every transaction on a calendar.
  • Your data is a plain text fileEdit it in the Raw text view, or download it whenever you like.

Reports and checks are run by hledger, right here in your browser.
